FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything decision-makers ask before rolling out MITANZ360Edu.

What exactly is MITANZ360Edu?
MITANZ360Edu is an AI-powered Digital Education Operating System that combines AI teaching, assessment, analytics and institutional workflow automation on Microsoft-native infrastructure.
How is this different from a traditional LMS like Moodle or Canvas?
Traditional LMS platforms manage courses and content. MITANZ360Edu is AI-first from the ground up, with native SharePoint, Microsoft Graph, Power Platform and Azure OpenAI integration — capabilities generic LMS platforms don't offer.
Do we need Microsoft 365 to use MITANZ360Edu?
MITANZ360Edu is optimised for institutions already using or planning to adopt Microsoft 365. Both Microsoft 365 A1 (education) and Business/Individual licensing are supported.
How is pricing structured?
Pricing is annual, institution-wide, and scales with student capacity — from 200 students (Starter Institute) up to 5,000+ students (University), with custom Enterprise pricing for larger or government deployments.
Can we start with a small pilot?
Yes. The Starter Institute tier is designed for smaller institutes and pilots, supporting up to 200 students and 20 staff with core AI, LMS and reporting features.
What's included in the Professional Institute tier?
All AI features, API integration, advanced analytics, student and teacher portals, and an optional white-label option — designed for mid-sized providers up to 1,000 students.
Is white labelling available?
Yes. White label branding is optional on the Professional Institute tier and included as standard on the University and Enterprise tiers.
How does the API integration mode work?
Partner institutes can embed MITANZ360Edu's AI directly into their own LMS, SIS or student portal via API — rather than replacing their existing systems outright.
What support is available?
Email support is included from the Starter tier, with priority support on Professional, SLA-backed support on University, and 24×7 premium support on Enterprise.
Is our data secure?
Yes. The platform uses Microsoft Entra ID authentication, MFA, encrypted connections, role-based access control and audit logging by default.
Can MITANZ360Edu handle multi-campus institutions?
Yes. The University tier includes native multi-campus support, single sign-on, and a dedicated database for larger, more complex institutions.
What implementation and migration support is offered?
One-time professional services cover implementation, data migration, staff training and custom development, so onboarding risk is minimised.
Can we integrate our existing Student Information System?
Yes, Student Information System integration is available as an add-on, alongside Microsoft 365/SharePoint integration and API expansion packs.
Does MITANZ360Edu support TVET and vocational training?
Yes. The platform is designed to serve private institutes, universities, TVET institutions, professional training providers, corporate and government training programmes alike.
